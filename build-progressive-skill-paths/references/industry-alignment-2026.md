# Industry Alignment Reference Set — 2026

This is a curated set of ten respected frameworks and resources, not an objective ranking. Some are formal or consensus-based professional frameworks; others provide authoritative curriculum, labor-market, or learning-design guidance. Apply only those relevant to the requested skill.

Verified on **2026-09-23** against the publishers' official pages.

## 1. SFIA 9

- **Publisher:** SFIA Foundation
- **Current reference:** SFIA 9, published October 2024 and marked current.
- **Use for:** professional skills, behavioral factors, and seven levels of responsibility from Follow through Set strategy/inspire/mobilise.
- **Apply:** map autonomy, influence, complexity, business skills, and accountability. Do not reduce SFIA levels to course difficulty.
- **Official source:** [SFIA 9](https://sfia-online.org/en/sfia-9) and [levels of responsibility](https://sfia-online.org/en/sfia-9/responsibilities)

## 2. DigComp 3.0

- **Publisher:** European Commission Joint Research Centre
- **Current reference:** DigComp 3.0, fifth edition (2025), the latest framework available in 2026.
- **Use for:** digital competence, including AI-related knowledge, skills, and attitudes across 21 competences.
- **Apply:** use its four proficiency levels—Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, Highly advanced—to calibrate task complexity and autonomy for digital topics.
- **Official source:** [DigComp 3.0](https://joint-research-centre.ec.europa.eu/scientific-activities/key-competences-lifelong-learning/digital-competence-framework-digcomp/digcomp-30_en)

## 3. ESCO v1.2.1

- **Publisher:** European Commission
- **Current reference:** ESCO v1.2.1, released December 2025.
- **Use for:** a multilingual vocabulary linking occupations, skills, competences, and qualifications.
- **Apply:** discover role-relevant skills and use consistent labels. Validate local job-market relevance rather than copying every related skill.
- **Official source:** [ESCO portal](https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en) and [v1.2.1 release](https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en/news/esco-v121-live)

## 4. NICE Workforce Framework for Cybersecurity

- **Publisher:** U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology
- **Current reference:** NIST SP 800-181 Rev. 1 with NICE Framework Components v2.2.0. NIST's official pages agree on the version but currently disagree on whether its April 28 release year is 2025 or 2026; cite the version and recheck the metadata when the date matters.
- **Use for:** cybersecurity work roles, competency areas, and Task, Knowledge, and Skill statements.
- **Apply:** convert target work into authentic cyber tasks and evidence. Use only for cybersecurity or strongly related work.
- **Official source:** [NICE Framework current versions](https://www.nist.gov/itl/applied-cybersecurity/nice/nice-framework-resource-center/nice-framework-current-versions) and [latest updates](https://www.nist.gov/itl/applied-cybersecurity/nice/nice-framework-resource-center/about/nice-framework-latest-updates)

## 5. European Qualifications Framework

- **Publisher:** European Union / Europass
- **Current reference:** eight learning-outcome levels.
- **Use for:** describing progression in knowledge, cognitive and practical skills, responsibility, and autonomy.
- **Apply:** write observable outcomes and distinguish supervised performance from independent and strategic performance.
- **Official source:** [EQF overview](https://europass.europa.eu/en/european-qualifications-framework-eqf) and [eight level descriptors](https://europass.europa.eu/en/description-eight-eqf-levels)

## 6. O*NET Content Model

- **Publisher:** U.S. Department of Labor-sponsored O*NET program
- **Current reference:** the maintained O*NET Content Model and current database.
- **Use for:** connecting worker characteristics and requirements with occupation-specific activities and work context.
- **Apply:** ground learning paths in real tasks, knowledge, skills, tools, and work context for a target occupation.
- **Official source:** [O*NET Content Model](https://www.onetcenter.org/content.html)

## 7. Computer Science Curricula 2023

- **Publisher:** ACM, IEEE Computer Society, and AAAI joint task force
- **Current reference:** CS2023 final report, endorsed in 2024.
- **Use for:** computer science knowledge areas, competency framing, curricular practices, professional dispositions, and societal/ethical context.
- **Apply:** define durable CS foundations and select depth by the learner's target competency area; do not treat every knowledge area as equally deep for every role.
- **Official source:** [CS2023](https://csed.acm.org/) and [final report](https://csed.acm.org/final-report/)

## 8. SWEBOK Guide v4.0a

- **Publisher:** IEEE Computer Society
- **Current reference:** SWEBOK Guide v4.0a, updated September 25, 2025.
- **Use for:** consensus-driven software engineering knowledge across 18 knowledge areas, including architecture, operations, security, Agile, and DevOps coverage.
- **Apply:** check that software-engineering paths cover lifecycle, quality, professional practice, and cross-cutting concerns—not coding alone.
- **Official source:** [IEEE Computer Society SWEBOK](https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering)

## 9. IES/What Works Clearinghouse learning practice guide

- **Publisher:** U.S. Institute of Education Sciences
- **Current reference:** *Organizing Instruction and Study to Improve Student Learning*.
- **Use for:** evidence-informed spacing, interleaving worked examples with problem solving, combining concrete and abstract representations, retrieval practice, and deep explanatory questions.
- **Apply:** structure concept modules and reviews. Treat evidence strength as described by the publisher and adapt beyond classroom contexts with judgment.
- **Official source:** [IES practice guide](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1)

## 10. OECD Learning Compass 2030

- **Publisher:** Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development
- **Current reference:** the evolving Learning Compass 2030 framework.
- **Use for:** learner agency plus knowledge, skills, attitudes, values, and transformative competencies.
- **Apply:** include self-direction, responsibility, collaboration, ethics, and the ability to navigate unfamiliar contexts.
- **Official source:** [OECD Learning Compass 2030](https://www.oecd.org/en/data/tools/oecd-learning-compass-2030.html)

## Alignment method

1. Choose the target occupation or use case.
2. Use ESCO or O*NET to identify real tasks and skill language.
3. Use the applicable body of knowledge: CS2023, SWEBOK, or NICE.
4. Use SFIA, DigComp, or EQF to calibrate levels of autonomy and complexity.
5. Use IES guidance to design practice and retrieval.
6. Use the OECD Learning Compass to check agency, attitudes, values, and wider responsibility.
7. State which references were used, which were not applicable, and the verification date.

Do not claim endorsement by any publisher. Respect each source's licensing and adaptation guidance.
